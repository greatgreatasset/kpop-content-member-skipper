Hearts2Hearts screen time

The first column is the time the add-on really jumps over in each video: only the parts where Carmen and/or Ye-on are on screen with nobody else. The member columns show how much of the video each member is on screen at all, alone or with others, so a group video can show Carmen at 90% and still have almost nothing skipped. "Time together" counts only the moments when exactly those members are recognized on screen. "If random" is how long they would share the screen if who appears with whom were random, given how often each member is in two-member (or three-member) shots at all. "vs random" is the first divided by the second: above 1 means they are shown together more than chance, below 1 less. "Videos" counts videos where they get at least 5 seconds together. A high number shows a pattern, not a reason: seating order, friendships, units and shared segments all push pairs together.
