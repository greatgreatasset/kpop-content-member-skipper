Pairs and trios use only full-group videos. "Time together" counts only the moments when exactly those members are recognized on screen. "If random" is how long they would share the screen if who appears with whom were random, given how often each member is in two-member (or three-member) shots at all. "vs random" is the first divided by the second, with one minute added to both so that pairs with very little footage can't show extreme ratios by chance: above 1 means they are shown together more than chance, below 1 less. "Videos" counts videos where they get at least 5 seconds together. A high number shows a pattern, not a reason: seating order, friendships, units and shared segments all push pairs together.
