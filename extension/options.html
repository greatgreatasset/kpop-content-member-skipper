"Main focus": hers is the largest face, clearly bigger (1.5×) than anyone else's, and near the middle of the picture. Fewer, shorter highlights; skipping is not affected. Older skip lists without focus info keep "any time" until they are rescanned.

When a member you skip is on screen together with someone you don't skip, that part can't be skipped; this blurs her face instead. Works for videos scanned with scanner 0.7.0 or later (others until they are rescanned). Not while an ad plays, and not when skipping is turned off.
